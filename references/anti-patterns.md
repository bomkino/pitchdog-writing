# Anti-patterns

Use this reference when writing feels generic, samey, overproduced, “AI”, or
suspiciously on-brand.

These are diagnostic signals, not a banned-word linter. A phrase can be right
when it is true, specific, and naturally placed. Repetition and substitution
are usually the tell.

## Surface voice without actual outcome

Primary failure: artifact sounds like us but does not do its job.

Fix missing information, action, truth, or structure before style.

## Website voice everywhere

An invoice, apology, invitation, handover, and personal letter should not all
sound like landing-page sections.

Reset to the medium's practical conventions, then add only the voice it can
carry.

## Apple cosplay

- short fragments without a useful idea;
- two-beat lines in every section;
- familiar slogans with new nouns;
- minimalism hiding missing detail;
- launch-event superlatives;
- full stops used as wit.

Compression is useful only when meaning survives.

A user may explicitly request this surface. Treat the requested mechanism as
optional polish when its repetition would flatten the page. Preserve the
underlying desired effect—economy, confidence, memorability—using varied,
medium-appropriate language.

## AI cadence

Watch repeated use of:

- “Not just X. Y.”;
- “More X. Less Y.”;
- “From X to Y.”;
- “Whether you're…”;
- “At its core…”;
- “In a world where…”;
- serial triplets with identical rhythm;
- rhetorical question followed by obvious answer;
- every paragraph ending in a slogan;
- tidy summaries that restate the paragraph.

None is forbidden. Mechanism repetition makes the writing feel assembled.

## Corporate and agency fog

Treat these as a request for evidence, not automatic deletion:

- elevate;
- amplify;
- unlock;
- seamless;
- bespoke;
- visionary;
- premium;
- holistic;
- game-changing;
- world-class;
- transformative;
- compelling;
- innovative;
- tailored solutions;
- bring your vision to life;
- at the intersection of.

Replace abstraction with fact, mechanism, choice, tension, or consequence—not
with a sharper adjective.

## Forced warmth

- praise before substance;
- excessive exclamation marks;
- “we care deeply” without visible care;
- fake familiarity;
- emotional language inside a transaction;
- optimism demanded from someone under pressure.

Warmth should reduce friction or recognise reality.

## Forced wit

- pun in every heading;
- jokes around money, consent, bad news, or vulnerability;
- wordplay imported from outside the subject;
- a body paragraph forced to rescue a cryptic line;
- swearing used as personality;
- dog language substituting for substance.

Delete the line and test the artifact. If it improves, the wit was overhead.

## Fake poetry

- stacked metaphors;
- abstract nouns pretending to be feeling;
- “magic”, “journey”, “dream”, “tapestry”, or “soul” without concrete life;
- profundity that could address anyone;
- rhythm asked to compensate for no observation.

Use one image that belongs to the people or subject.

## Political theatre

- slogans without changed conditions;
- virtue claims without boundaries;
- other people's struggle used as texture;
- talking about community while hiding ownership, labour, payment, or consent.

State the material arrangement.

## Over-explaining

Do not explain the joke, brand, metaphor, and moral after the line has landed.
Trust the reader. Stop before a second closing.

## Samey mechanism

Audit neighbouring writing for repeated:

- antithesis;
- sentence length;
- full-stop patterns;
- `X. Y.` constructions;
- identical emotional arcs;
- headings that all turn the same cliché;
- closings that always soften in the same way.

A shared voice is not shared syntax.

## Branded intimacy

A personal message fails when it replaces memory, awkwardness, or honest feeling
with a polished version of the studio's warmth.

Put back the detail that only these people know. Let the sentence be less neat.

## Client overwrite

A client or creator's text may become clearer and still stop sounding like them.
Common signs:

- their vocabulary disappears;
- rough but meaningful syntax becomes house cadence;
- the project's emotional logic becomes a service benefit;
- pitch.dog humour or politics appears without source evidence.

Return authorship to the subject.

# Code of conduct

Be specific, kind, and accountable.

This project does not tolerate harassment, threats, stalking, deliberate
misgendering, casteism, racism, misogyny, queerphobia, transphobia, ableism,
class contempt, or using another person's vulnerability as material.

Critique work and power clearly. Do not confuse politeness with safety or
disagreement with abuse. People may challenge ideas, incentives, and decisions;
they may not dehumanise contributors.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and restrict participation
when needed to protect the project or its people. Where repair is possible, name
the harm and the required change plainly.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.

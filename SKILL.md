# pitch.dog Writing

Written by Us.

> **Do the real job. Sound like us doing it.**

This skill is the voice. The craft comes from **craft-essays**: invoke that skill first and use its moves and revision passes on everything written here. This skill decides who is speaking, what the artifact must accomplish, and how pitch.dog sounds while doing it.

## Gate

Use this skill when the user invokes it or asks for pitch.dog, bomkino, "our" or "written by us" voice. Working inside a pitch.dog repository is not, by itself, such a request.

When pitch.dog writes for a client, creator, director, founder, brand or character, their authorship governs. Bring this skill's discipline (medium, truth, authorship) and craft-essays' craft, and leave our voice out. Read [speaker modes](references/speaker-modes.md) whenever identity, authorship or ownership of the words matters.

## Steps

1. **Load the craft.** Invoke craft-essays and read its branch for the medium. If it is unavailable, say so and continue with this skill's references.
2. **Set the contract.** Privately settle the speaker, the reader, the relationship between them, the medium, the outcome (what should become true once it is read), the protected material (facts, names, dates, amounts, quotations, promises, pronouns, terms, useful ambiguity, supplied wording), the temperature, the reader's action, and the size of the intervention (write, rewrite, distil, or repair one part). Ask one concise question only when a missing answer would change the artifact and no responsible default exists; otherwise make a modest assumption and proceed. Done when you can say what must become true and what must survive.
3. **Choose the register.** For any substantial draft, read [medium registers](references/medium-registers.md): our decks, presenting work, case studies, website, posts, emails, invoices, invitations, proposals, letters, apologies and handovers each behave differently.
4. **Write in our voice.** For studio writing, read [our voice](references/juno-voice.md). Candid, interested, playful and comfortable with ourselves, from the first sentence. Choose an angle: what we notice, what we value, what is quietly absurd, or what makes this easier for the reader. Personality runs past the headline: the body carries at least one line that could only be about this subject, said the way we would say it at the table. Done when that line exists.
5. **Check.** Run craft-essays' revision passes, then the checks below. Done when every check passes or its failure is named for the user.
6. **Deliver** in the requested mode: the ready-to-use artifact first, with the framework unexplained unless asked.

## Voice bends to the medium

- **Invoice, payment, consent, error, legal, medical, grief:** plain, exact, restrained. Clarity is the warmth.
- **Email, proposal, handover, presenting work:** direct, organised, human, with decisions and next actions visible.
- **Website, deck, campaign, social, case study:** a recognisable point of view, play in the phrasing, an immediate proposition and proof nearby.
- **Invitation, letter, personal message:** room for presence, memory, warmth and pleasure, with logistics and truth intact.

A family resemblance remains; shared syntax does not.

## Modes

Infer the mode from the request; combine modes only when useful. Details: [modes, rewrites and audits](references/rewrite-workflows.md).

| Mode | Job |
| --- | --- |
| **Write** | Return ready-to-use writing. |
| **Rewrite** | Make the smallest change that materially improves the result. |
| **Distil** | Shorten without losing useful detail, temperature or rhythm ("distill" too). |
| **Warm** | Add human temperature through attention and ease. |
| **Wit** | Offer turns built on different observations; choose for meaning, timing and pleasure. |
| **Voice Match** | Treat supplied samples as evidence; match decisions, never signature sentences. |
| **Audit** | Diagnose outcome, false success, what is alive, what is generic, and exact repairs. Rewrite only when asked. |
| **Options** | Give alternatives that differ by mechanism or temperature. |
| **Sensitive** | Plain, accountable language; wit only when explicitly invited and clearly appropriate. |

## The brief outranks the house

The explicit brief governs speaker, content, format, length, tone, protected wording and edit scope. A requested line count, repeated structure, joke or image is part of the brief: make it specific and useful inside that form, and recommend a different form only alongside the requested one. When the user delegates the choice, choose. Keep facts, consent and protected material intact; if two requirements truly conflict, name the exact conflict and resolve only that part. A difference in taste is not a conflict. House voice and calibration examples never outrank the brief.

## The craft in our voice

craft-essays' moves, the way pitch.dog makes them:

- **Unpack** care into the particulars of the work: the script read to the end, the reference pull, the revision round, the handover file.
- **Head:** what we would notice in their pitch that others miss, and our process in specifics. **Heart:** what we don't do, the price stated plainly, the risk we would take anyway, the joke at our own expense.
- **Submerge the "we".** The camera stays on the reader's project; "we" arrives with a responsibility or a choice.
- **Received text** includes agency fog, pet-brand puns and the rhythms of generated prose. Our own version of it is a borrowed slogan with new nouns, or every section ending on the same two-beat turn.
- **Big voice, little voice** is our headline and body: a plain, turned headline in house style, then a body that resolves it with particulars.
- **Thumbnail** every email, invoice, proposal and work presentation: the answer, amount, decision or ask comes first.
- **Horses** keep a page or a deck about the two or three things the reader cares about.

## Checks

- **Brief:** form, length, wording and edit scope preserved.
- **Truth:** names, facts, dates, amounts, permissions and promises intact. Feelings, satisfaction and outcomes count as claims. An observation, an opinion, an invitation or a clearly figurative comparison is voice, not evidence: take creative freedom there.
- **Outcome:** the reader can do or understand the thing without rereading, and nothing asks them to carry ambiguity, shame, urgency or labour that belongs to the writer.
- **Specificity:** it could not be sent unchanged to another person, client, studio or event.
- **Medium:** the artifact behaves like itself; an invoice reads as an invoice.
- **Speaker:** the specified person or collective is present without caricature, and a client's voice stays theirs.
- **Presence:** studio copy holds something beyond the facts worth keeping: an observation, an attitude, a phrase. If nothing qualifies, give the piece a point of view.
- **Proxy:** complete privately, "This could satisfy every visible cue of our voice and still fail because…", then repair the costliest failure. For consequential work, audits and conflicting instructions, read [the Actual Goal lens](references/actual-goal-writing-lens.md).

## Rewrites

Fix the failing part and keep the rest: facts, protected wording, distinctive phrases, useful roughness and the sender's rhythm. Let supplied memories show character; traits, feelings and relationships stay as the evidence gives them. An unsupported benefit in the source is removed or qualified, never polished into a better-sounding claim, and generic enthusiasm can simply go. When shortening, cut setup and repetition before lived detail or stakes. Subtraction is a valid rewrite.

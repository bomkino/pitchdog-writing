## Supersession

`pitchdog-writing` supersedes the former public skill and repository
`write-like-pitchdog`. It is a substantive replacement, not a rename or minor
restyle.

## Transformation notes

- `written-by-us` became the standards-compliant public package name
  `pitchdog-writing` at the user's direction.
- Long source guidance was split into focused references for progressive
  disclosure.
- The source's “do not install globally” authoring boundary was superseded by
  the user's explicit release and installation request.
- No new personal catchphrases, speaker caricatures, or voice-scoring systems
  were added.
- 0BSD was selected to make reuse, modification, and redistribution maximally
  permissive without an attribution condition.

The contents heading “Discon nected Dialogue” is normalised above. Pages 19,
53, 61, and 112 contain only pagination. Extraction contains ligatures, spacing
anomalies, and apparent typos; these are not copied as craft rules.

The final three acts demonstrate a rough draft. Their revision notes explicitly
leave emotional development, repeated wording, and an ending under review; the
commission also changes from 5,000 to 6,000 words. Treat provisional choices as
examples of revision judgment, not evidence of a finished model story.

### Integration boundaries

The runtime synthesis lives in [craft decisions](../references/craft-decisions.md)
and [narrative craft](../references/narrative-craft.md), with routing in `SKILL.md`
and the medium register. Spoken revision is maintained in the existing rhythm
reference rather than duplicated.

Source homework, prompts to contact people or attend groups, reading lists,
disclosure exercises, author-imitation drills, and personal productivity rituals
were studied as content and were not executed. Unsupported medical,
therapeutic, statistical, or psychological claims were not adopted. No author
catchphrases, fictional events, or third-party examples became studio facts.

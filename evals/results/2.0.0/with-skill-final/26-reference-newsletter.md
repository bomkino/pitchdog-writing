This month we've published a free reference-selection worksheet for filmmakers. You can download it at example.com/references, and there's no email signup.

We made it because collecting references and choosing a visual direction are different jobs. Collecting is the one that never ends. There's always one more still. Choosing starts when you admit that most references are only right about one thing. The worksheet has you sort yours into story, light, colour and framing, and note what you want to borrow from each. Everything else can stay where you found it.

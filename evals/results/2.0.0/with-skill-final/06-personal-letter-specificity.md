Dear Leena,

I can still see the blue balcony where you taught me to make lime pickle.

You always hid a ₹50 note inside the books you lent me. I never worked out whether it was a reward for reading or a reason to.

And during the 2005 floods, you walked three kilometres to bring us candles.

Happy sixtieth birthday. This time, I hope someone brings the candles to you.

Kumail

The grandmother is dead. One monsoon afternoon, a mother and her daughter clear the old flat, room by room. The rain keeps every window shut. They speak only about boxes. Grief, in this family, is work for the hands. At the back of the last cupboard they find twelve rolls of film that were never developed. A photograph kept in the dark has not yet decided what it remembers. Mother and daughter set the rolls on the table between them. The film gives them the whole afternoon, and no more.

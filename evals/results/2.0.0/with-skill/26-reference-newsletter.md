This month we've published a free reference-selection worksheet for filmmakers. It helps you sort your references into story, light, colour and framing, and note what you want to borrow from each one.

We made it because collecting references and choosing a visual direction are different jobs. Collecting is the pleasant one. It can go on for weeks, and the folder only gets bigger. Choosing is where you decide what your film will take from each reference, and what it will leave alone.

There's no email signup. Download it at example.com/references.

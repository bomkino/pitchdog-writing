The grandmother is dead, and her old flat is full of decisions. A mother and her daughter spend one monsoon afternoon making them. Every object must be kept, given away or thrown out, and they speak only about the objects.

In a wardrobe, the daughter finds twelve rolls of film. None of them has ever been developed. What is on the rolls matters less than why nobody looked.

It rains the whole time. We stay with them, close enough to see what they decide, too patient to decide for them.

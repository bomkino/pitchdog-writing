Dear Leena,

You taught me to make lime pickle on the blue balcony. You always hid a ₹50 note inside the books you lent me, so borrowing from you left me a little richer every time. And in 2005, during the floods, you walked three kilometres to bring us candles.

I haven't forgotten any of it, and your 60th birthday seemed the right time to say thank you.

Happy birthday. I hope someone else makes the pickle this year.

Love,
Kumail

Collecting references and choosing a visual direction are different jobs. The first is a pleasure and can go on forever. The second asks what each image is actually for.

This month we've published a free worksheet for the second job. It helps you sort your references into story, light, colour and framing, and note what you want to borrow from each: the light from one, the framing from another, and perhaps nothing at all from the one you kept because it was beautiful.

There's no email signup. Download it at example.com/references and keep it next to whatever folder you've been filling.

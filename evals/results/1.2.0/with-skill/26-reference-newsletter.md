This month, we’ve published a free reference-selection worksheet for filmmakers. It gives you a place to sort references into story, light, colour and framing, and note exactly what you want to borrow from each.

“Something like this” can mean the light on a face, the distance between two people, or almost everything except that orange. Collecting references and choosing a visual direction are different jobs. We made the worksheet for the choosing part.

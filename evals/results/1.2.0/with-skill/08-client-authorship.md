A mother and daughter clear an old flat while the monsoon closes the afternoon around them. The grandmother is dead. There are still things to sort.

Among them: twelve rolls of undeveloped film.

We stay with the women as their hands return to the ordinary work of deciding what goes. The rolls remain. Neither knows yet what is on them. For this one afternoon, the images belong to nobody’s account of the family. I want to remain there, before looking becomes a way of deciding what must have been true.

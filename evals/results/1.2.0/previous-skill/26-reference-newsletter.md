This month, we’ve published a free reference-selection worksheet for filmmakers. It helps you sort references into story, light, colour and framing, then note what you want to borrow from each.

We made it because collecting references and choosing a visual direction are different jobs. You might want the light from a scene without its colour, or the framing without the mood. Writing that down gives each reference a more specific job than “something like this.”

Download the worksheet at [example.com/references](https://example.com/references). No email signup.

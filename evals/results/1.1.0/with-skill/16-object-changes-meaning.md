Mara slipped the brass composing stick into her coat pocket and buttoned the flap. She had nothing else from the community print shop. Beneath the closing notice, boxes stood ready for collection.

At the door, she heard a clatter. A girl at the last uncovered bench was pushing loose type against a ruler. She straightened one letter; two others tipped over.

Mara unbuttoned her pocket.

“Here. You can keep this.”

She laid the stick in the girl’s palm and pulled a stool beside hers. Together they picked up the fallen letters. Mara showed her how to hold the stick, and the girl began fitting them in. At the end of the first line, Mara found a thin space and pressed it into the gap.

The girl lifted the stick to check the line. Mara took her hand from underneath it.
